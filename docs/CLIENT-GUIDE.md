# Your website inquiries: how to use the Google Sheet

When a customer fills in the **Request a Quote** form on your website, their request appears
in your Google Sheet automatically. You don't need to log in to the website or install anything.

## Where to find inquiries

1. Open the Google Sheet we shared with you (bookmark it on your phone and computer).
2. Click the **Inquiries** tab at the bottom.
3. Each new request is a new row at the bottom. The newest are at the bottom.

You can also use the Google Sheets app on your phone.

## What each column means

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Timestamp | When the customer sent the request (Philippine time). |
| Inquiry ID | The reference number the customer was shown, e.g. `LAU-20261006-7KQ2M`. Ask for it when they call. |
| Full Name, Phone, Email | How to reach the customer. Email may be empty. |
| Service | The service they chose. |
| Estimated Amount | Their own estimate, for example "2 bags". |
| Service Type | Drop-off, Pickup, Delivery, or Pickup & Delivery. |
| Address | Only filled in for pickup or delivery. |
| Preferred Date / Time | When they'd like it. These may be empty. |
| Message | Special instructions. |
| Status | Where the inquiry is. **This is the column for you to update.** |

## Tracking an inquiry

Click the **Status** cell and pick from the list:

- **New**: just arrived, nobody has replied yet
- **Contacted**: you've called or messaged the customer
- **Quoted**: you've given them a price
- **Confirmed**: they said yes
- **Completed**: the laundry is done and returned
- **Cancelled**: they decided not to go ahead

Tip: click the filter icon on the Status header to show only **New** inquiries.

## Getting an email when a new inquiry arrives

In the sheet: **Tools → Notification settings → Edit notifications → "Any changes are made" →
"Email – right away" → Save**.

## Please don't

- **Rename the "Inquiries" tab or change the words in row 1.** The website writes into those
  exact columns, and new inquiries will stop arriving if they change.
- **Delete row 1.**
- **Share the sheet with "Anyone with the link".** It holds customers' phone numbers and
  addresses. Share it only with staff, by their email address.

You can add your own columns to the **right of Status** (for example "Price" or "Notes").
That's safe.

## Looking after customer data

The website's privacy notice tells customers their details are only used to answer their
request. Every few months, delete or move out old inquiries you no longer need.

## If inquiries stop arriving

Customers can still call or message you. The website shows them your number if the form
can't send. Contact your web developer and mention the time it stopped.

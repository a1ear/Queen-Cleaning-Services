# Your admin panel: how to manage requests

When a customer fills in the **Request Services** form on your website, the request appears
in your admin panel straight away. You don't need any other app.

## Signing in

1. Go to **your-website.com/admin** (bookmark it on your phone and computer).
2. Enter your email and password, then **Sign in**. You stay signed in for 14 days on that device.
3. When you're done on a shared computer, click **Sign out** at the top.

Only your admin email can sign in. If you forget the password, contact your web developer
(see "If something goes wrong" below).

## The three screens

| Screen | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Inquiries** | Every request from the website. Opens on **New** so you see what needs a reply first. The number next to "Inquiries" at the top is how many are still New. |
| **Schedule** | Upcoming cleaning jobs, grouped by day, from today onward. |
| **Customers** | Everyone who has sent a request. People are matched by phone number, so a repeat client shows all their requests together. |

The panel updates by itself. A new request appears without refreshing the page.

## Handling a new request

1. Open **Inquiries** and tap the request.
2. Use the buttons at the top: **Call**, **Text**, **Email**, or **Map** (opens the address in Google Maps).
3. Change the **Status** as you go:
   - **New**: just arrived, nobody has replied yet
   - **Contacted**: you've called or messaged the customer
   - **Quoted**: you've given them a price
   - **Confirmed**: they said yes
   - **Completed**: the cleaning is done
   - **Cancelled**: they decided not to go ahead
4. **Quote:** type the amount in pesos and what it covers, then **Save quote**. If the request
   is still New or Contacted, it moves to **Quoted** automatically.
5. **Cleaning schedule:** pick the date and time, then **Save schedule**. The job now shows on
   the **Schedule** screen.
6. **Add a note** for anything worth remembering, such as "prefers mornings, has a dog".

## The history

Every request keeps a **History** at the bottom: when it arrived, every status change, every
quote (with the old and new amount), every schedule change, and every note, with the time.
Nothing in the history can be edited or deleted, so you always have a record of what was agreed.

The **Request** box shows exactly what the customer typed. It can't be edited either. Use a
note if something they wrote turns out to be wrong.

## Searching

Use the search box on **Inquiries** to find a request by name, phone number, reference number
(for example `QC-20261014-7KQ2M`), or address. Customers will often quote the reference number
from their confirmation screen.

## Looking after customer data

The panel holds customers' names, phone numbers and addresses. Keep your password private,
don't sign in on computers you don't trust, and sign out on shared devices. If a customer
asks you to delete their details, contact your web developer.

## If something goes wrong

- **The form stopped working:** customers still see your phone number on the website, so they
  can call. Contact your web developer and mention when it started.
- **You forgot your password, or think someone else knows it:** contact your web developer
  to reset it.
